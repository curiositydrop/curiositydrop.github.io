Why BANDtroductions Exists

The local music scene is full of incredible talent — but it isn’t always easy for bands, musicians, venues, and fans to find each other.

BANDtroductions was created to solve that by building a central hub where:

  • 🎸 Bands can get discovered
  • 🥁 Musicians can find bands
  • 🎤 Venues can easily discover local talent
  • 🎶 Fans can explore and support local music

This platform is completely free for bands, musicians, and venues to join.

Our Mission

BANDtroductions exists to strengthen and spotlight Maine’s local music scene by creating a platform built for the people who keep it alive — the bands, musicians, venues, and fans.

Our mission is simple: make it easier for local artists to be discovered, supported, promoted, and connected without charging them just to participate.

We believe local music deserves more visibility, more opportunity, and a stronger sense of community. Every profile created, every show discovered, every fan interaction, and every local business sponsor helps push the scene forward.

This isn’t just another music website — it’s a growing hub built to support the people creating the soundtrack of Maine.
