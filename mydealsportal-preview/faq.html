Frequently asked questions

Simple on purpose.

How much does it cost a business?

$50 per month for one business profile and unlimited legitimate deals.

Does MyDealsPortal take a commission?

No. The platform is built around a flat monthly advertising fee rather than taking a percentage of each customer transaction.

How many deals can I post?

As many legitimate offers as you want while your subscription is active.

Do deals need approval?

Normal business deals publish without an approval queue. MyDealsPortal still reserves the ability to remove fraud, abuse, illegal content, impersonation, or spam.

How do consumers search?

They can search by keyword, ZIP code, category, or combine keyword and ZIP for a more specific result.

Can I edit or pause a deal?

Yes. Businesses control their own deals from the self-service dashboard.

Do customers need an account?

No account is planned to be required simply to browse and discover public deals.
