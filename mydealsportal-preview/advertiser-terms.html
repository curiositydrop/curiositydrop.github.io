Business rules

Advertiser Terms

Working launch draft

$50 monthly subscription

A paid business subscription is intended to include one business profile and unlimited legitimate active deals for that business during an active billing period.

Unlimited does not mean spam

Duplicate, misleading, keyword-stuffed, irrelevant, fake, or abusive listings may be removed. Businesses must only advertise offers they are authorized and able to honor.

Business responsibility

Advertisers are responsible for all deal details, pricing, eligibility, restrictions, taxes, fulfillment, customer service, and compliance with laws applicable to their products, services, and advertising.

Deal changes

Businesses may create, edit, pause, reactivate, or remove their own deals while their subscription is active.

Cancellation

Subscriptions are intended to renew monthly until canceled. Cancellation stops future renewal; exact billing and refund language will match the production payment configuration at launch.

Removal rights

MyDealsPortal may suspend or remove accounts or deals involving fraud, impersonation, illegal activity, repeated consumer complaints, security risks, or other serious abuse.

Final billing/refund wording will be synchronized with the Stripe subscription settings before accepting payment.
